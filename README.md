# Supply Chain Management Portfolio

Welcome to my Supply Chain Management Portfolio!

This repository showcases my projects, practical analyses, and business case studies related to Supply Chain Management, Supply Chain Analytics, and operational improvement.

As an Industrial Engineering graduate, I am interested in applying analytical and problem-solving skills to improve supply chain operations, optimize resources, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Areas of Interest

- **Inventory Management:** Inventory Control, ABC Analysis, XYZ Analysis, Safety Stock, and Reorder Point.
- **Warehouse Management:** Warehouse Operations, Inventory Accuracy, Storage Optimization, and Productivity Analysis.
- **Procurement & Purchasing:** Supplier Evaluation, Lead Time Analysis, and Procurement Performance.
- **Logistics & Distribution:** Transportation Management, Delivery Performance, and Distribution Optimization.
- **Production Planning:** Material Requirement Planning (MRP), Production Scheduling, and Capacity Planning.
- **Supply Chain Analytics:** Supply Chain KPI Monitoring, Data Analysis, and Business Intelligence.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel – Data Analysis, Pivot Tables, Power Query, and Inventory Management.
- SQL – Data Extraction, Transformation, and Analysis.
- Python – Data Cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis, and Statistical Analysis.
- Power BI – Data Visualization, KPI Monitoring, and Interactive Dashboards.

## Repository Structure

Projects are organized by business functions and analytical topics, including datasets, analysis scripts, dashboards, documentation, and business recommendations.

## Project Approach

Each project follows an end-to-end analytical approach:

1. Business Understanding
2. Data Collection & Preparation
3. Exploratory Data Analysis
4. Supply Chain Performance Analysis
5. KPI Development & Visualization
6. Business Insights & Recommendations

## Objective

To continuously develop my expertise in Supply Chain Management and Data Analytics by transforming operational data into meaningful insights and actionable business recommendations.

*Note: Some projects use synthetic datasets and fictional business cases for learning and portfolio demonstration purposes.*
